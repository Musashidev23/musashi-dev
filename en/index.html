(01) How it works

Tell me what you sell, review a live preview on your phone, and ask for changes. Your site goes live on your own domain in 3 to 10 business days.

Four steps

by email or a quick call

  1. You tell me

    Your business name, what you sell and where you are. Photos of your place and your work make the site look like yours, not a template.

  2. You get a preview

    A live link you can open on your phone, so you see exactly how it looks before anything goes public.

  3. You ask for changes

    Swap a photo, fix a price, reword a line. Two rounds of changes are included.

(05) FAQ

Questions from business owners

01

I already have Facebook and Google. Do I need a website?

Social media works for people who already know you. A website shows up when someone searches “plumber near me”, and it makes your Google Business profile look trustworthy enough to call.

02

You're in Brazil. How does that work?

Everything happens by email, text or a short video call, in English. São Paulo is only 1 to 2 hours ahead of New York, so I'm working during your business day.

03

What do I need to send to get started?

Your business name, what you offer, your service area and any photos you have. If you have a logo, great. If not, I'll set up a clean wordmark.

04

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the final payment is in, the site and the domain are yours. If you ever want to move it, I hand over all the files.

05

How long does it take?

3 to 10 business days, depending on the size. The clock starts once I have your content.

06

Is the care plan required?

No. It's $79 a month and optional. It covers hosting, security updates and small edits, like new hours or prices.

07

Can I pay monthly instead?

Yes. For a One Page site it's $99 to start, then $79 a month with a 12-month minimum. Hosting and small changes are included, and after 12 months the site is yours.
